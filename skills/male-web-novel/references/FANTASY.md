# Fantasy Brain Holes (玄幻脑洞)

## Overview
Stories set in fantasy/martial arts worlds (仙侠、玄幻). Protagonist typically has system or game-like cheat ability. Fast-paced, power-progression driven.

## Key Elements
- **Background**: Xianxia,玄幻 cultivation worlds
- **Core Tone**: Fast, power-driven, cultivation/leveling focus
- **Characteristic**: Heavy RPG/game elements, absurd power growth

## Common Subgenres

### Invincible Check-in (无敌签到文)
**Core**: Protagonist "checks in" (签到) at different locations to obtain instant rewards

**Cheat Mechanism**:
- "Check in at sect → receive 10,000 years of cultivation"
- "Check in at ancient battlefield → god-tier weapon"
- "Daily check-in system with escalating rewards"
- "First visit reward: breakthrough to next realm"

**Progression**: New location → check-in → obtain reward → power up → explore stronger realm → repeat

**Tropes**: Underestimated protagonist vs. arrogant elders, rapid level-up, "trash-to-god" trope

### 10,000x Return (万倍返还文)
**Core**: Protagonist gets 10,000x return/gain on everything given away

**Cheat Mechanism**:
- "Gift a spiritual herb → 10,000x returned"
- "Teach technique → 10,000x cultivation comprehension returned"
- "Donate artifact → 10,000x stronger version returned"
- "Help someone receive luck/fate → 10,000x luck returned"

**Tension Setup**: Initially weak but giving away resources; sudden explosive growth when 10,000x activates

**Themes**: Benevolence pays off, surprise reveals, hidden power

### Immortality Hiding (长生苟道流)
**Core**: Protagonist has infinite lifespan but chooses cautious/safe path rather than reckless cultivation

**Protagonist Personality**:
- "Hide power, accumulate slowly" (苟)
- Avoid attention and conflict
- Watch events unfold from sidelines
- Strategic, calculated risk-taking

**Cheat Examples**:
- "Infinite lifespan" (don't die from age)
- "System giving safe rewards for hiding"
- "Can transfer between identities/bodies"
- "Perfect recovery and revival ability"

**Appeal**: Long-term planning, seeing history unfold, patient accumulation vs. reckless competitors

### Sect Building (宗门建设文)
**Core**: Protagonist rebuilds or grows a declining sect; protagonist's strength grows with sect's power

**Mechanics**:
- Start with poor/dangerous sect
- Recruit disciples, build facilities, restore reputation
- Protagonist grows faster as sect flourishes
- Usually includes: disciple talent triggers, resources accumulation, prestige system

**Tropes**:
- Disciple gets lucky/strong → sect gains fame
- Hidden enemies plotting against sect
- Inter-sect conflicts and tournaments
- Ancient secrets buried in sect grounds
- Genius disciples seeking shelter

### Disciples for Power (收徒就变强)
**Core**: Protagonist gains cultivation/stats by accepting disciples; talented disciples grant special bonuses

**Cheat Mechanism**:
- "Every disciple accepted +1 major cultivation realm"
- "Disciple has special physique/affinity → triggers bonus"
- "Disciple achieves breakthroughs → protagonist gains partial reward"
- "Disciple count determines power"

**Common Disciple Traits**:
- Rare affinity (fire, ice, void, time, etc.)
- Forbidden techniques/heritage
- God-killing physique
- Ancient soul/possession
- Rebirth knowledge

## Creative Guidelines

### For New Authors
**Advise**: Master existing tropes first (check-in, return, etc.).

**Why**: Established patterns have proven reader appeal; easier to learn structure before innovating

**Learn from**: Top-performing works in each subgenre; analyze pacing, conflict design, emotional hooks

### For Experienced Authors
**Advise**: Innovate on top of mature patterns; mix and match, introduce new angles

**Examples of Innovation**:
- Mix [Sect Building] + [Check-in]: Check-in at historic sect locations
- Mix [Return] + [Hiding]: Give away resources secretly, grow while anonymous
- Mix [Disciples] + [Time-travel]: Disciples all have cheat abilities from future

### Beyond Pure System-Power Fantasy
While system-based power leveling dominates, alternative approaches exist:

#### Character-Driven Martial Arts
- Focus on martial arts techniques, sword intent, battle philosophy
- Emphasis on teacher/student relationships, peer rivalries, sect politics
- Less "sudden god-tier rewards," more hard training and breakthrough moments

#### Comedy/Purple Haze (Playful/Witty) Fantasy
- Main appeal is humor, jokes, banter, ridiculous situations
- Characters have exaggerated personalities; power is secondary to fun dialogue
- Self-aware tropes, meta-humor
- "Playing with tropes" rather than strictly following them

### World-building Depth (for quality/immersiveness)
Don't neglect world-building details even in fast-paced fiction:

**Cultivation Systems**:
- Clear realm hierarchy (Qi Condensation → Foundation → Golden Core → Nascent Soul etc.)
- Unique breakthrough challenges per realm
- Society organized by power levels
- Resource scarcity and competition dynamics

**Power Sources**:
- Qi, spiritual energy, mana, etc. — define what powers the world
- Relationship between environment and cultivation (locations with denser energy, rare areas)
- Ancient wars shaping present geography

**Sect/Power Structures**:
- Sect hierarchies and internal politics
- Different cultivation philosophies (righteous vs. demonic, hardline vs. liberal)
- Sacred grounds, forbidden areas, ancient ruins

**Unique World Rules (Setting "Brain Holes")**:
- "Only single-horned unicorns can cultivate"
- "Must meditate under starlight; night = stronger, day = weaker"
- "Cultivation requires human life as fuel" (dark twist, ethical conflict)
- "Reincarnation cycles; can kill someone by destroying their past life records"

## Quality Improvements

**Make it feel "premium"** (质感):

1. **Characterization**:
   - Protagonist with personality beyond power hunger
   - Meaningful side characters with distinct traits
   - Antagonists with understandable motivations (not just evil for evil's sake)

2. **Emotional arc beyond power**:
   - Love interests (romance subplots)
   - Friendships and loyalty
   - Betrayal and loss
   - Moral dilemmas

3. **Pacing**:
   - Variety in conflict types (not all tournaments/power-level checks)
   - Exploration + story beats (secrets, legacies, ancient plots)
   - Character downtime (emotional moments, growth outside combat)

## Platform Notes
- **Encouraged**: Creative variations on existing patterns, character depth, rich world-building
- **Avoid**: Generic copy-paste without unique twist; completely abandoning genre tropes (readers expect certain patterns)
