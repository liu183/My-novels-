# Urban Brain Holes (都市脑洞)

## Overview
Stories set in modern urban/metro settings, focused on imagination and creativity. Protagonist has cheat abilities (systems, special powers, mutations). Rhythm is fast-paced and satisfying (爽快).

## Key Elements
- **Background**: Urban/metropolitan life
- **Core Tone**: Imagination-driven, quick-paced
- **Cheat Types**: Systems, supernatural abilities, body mutations, inherited powers

## Common Subgenres

### Wealth/God-tycoon (神豪类)
**Core**: Protagonist has wealth-creation cheat ability; getting rich is main storyline

**Cheat Examples**:
- "Get $10,000 every time someone compliments you"
- "Stock market prediction system"
- "Invest and get 10,000x return system"
- "Antique identification and valuation ability"

**Setting choices**: School, workplace, business circles, family dynamics

### Live Streaming (直播类)
**Core**: Livestreaming is main storyline with supernatural influence

**Cheat Examples**:
- "See viewers' bad luck aura; harvest resentment to strengthen"
- "Broadcast locations and events gain viewers' belief power"
- "Special effects: ghosts appear on camera when streaming"
- "Stream in supernatural settings (haunted houses, forbidden realms)"

**Tropes**: Mystery, supernatural encounters, ghost hunting, urban legends

### Arts & Entertainment (文娱类)
**Core**: Protagonist works in film/music/culture industry with career-advantage cheat

**Roles**: Screenwriter, director, singer, producer, influencer

**Cheat Examples**:
- "Future classics database system" (know future hit films/songs)
- "Instant mastery of any instrument/skill"
- "Audience emotion reading system"
- "Directors: can direct scenes with photorealistic preview"

**Settings**: Film studios, TV stations (卫视), record labels, social media platforms

### Food/Gastronomy (美食类)
**Core**: Food is central element with supernatural twists

**Cheat Examples**:
- "Recipes that grant buffs (strength, beauty, health)"
- "System rewarding trying rare/delicious foods"
- "Cooking with spirits/ghosts as customers"
- "Food that solves emotional problems"

**Settings**: Restaurants, food stalls, cooking competitions, street food culture

### Pet-focused (宠物类)
**Core**: Relationship with special pets drives story

**Cheat Examples**:
- "Communicate with pets; pets can teach skills"
- "System for raising/pet evolution"
- "Animals reveal secrets/clues"
- "Pets have human-like intelligence"

**Common Pets**: Spirit beasts in urban disguise, mutated animals, mythical creatures disguised as pets

### School Flower/Beauty (校花类)
**Core**: Interaction with beautiful classmates/spin-off from beauty-related conflicts

**Cheat Examples**: Varies; usually paired with other subgenre (school + gaming, school + supernatural)

**Tropes**: Competitions, love interests (harem or single), bullying vs. comeuppance, college life

## Creative Guidelines

### Multidimensional Growth
Protagonist development should span multiple dimensions instead of just strength:
- **Love**: Romantic relationships, dating
- **Career/Achievement**: Entrepreneurship, influencer success
- **Wealth**: Financial growth, luxury cars/houses
- **Social Status**: Fame, recognition

### World-building in Urban Settings
Even ordinary settings need fresh angles:
- Discover supernatural layer in normal life
- Hidden communities (cultivators in city, underground powers)  
- Special abilities secretly spreading
- Parallel dimensions bleeding into reality
- Modern technology + ancient/traditional powers

### Inspiration Sources
- **Hot trends**: Short video memes, celebrity gossip, workplace drama
- **Urban legends/ghost stories**: Adapt local spooky stories
- **Current events**: News disasters, viral events, social phenomena
- **Tech trends**: AI, VR, crypto, quantum computing, space exploration

### Avoid
- **Overused tropes without innovation**: Standard rebirth-to-school is cliché; add unique twist
- **Pure power fantasy without novelty**: Being strong is boring; HOW you get strong matters
- **Stiff, unnatural dialogue**: Keep modern urban speech natural for genre authenticity

## Platform-Specific Notes (Fanqie Novel/番茄小说)
- **Encouraged**: Creative thinking, multi-angle brain holes, logical consistency
- **Not encouraged**: Strategy guides/harem/posting/lazy plots
- **Copyright warning**: No political figures or real celebrity parodies in arts/entertainment genres
- **Cultivation/High-Fantasy**: Urban cultivation/wuxia stories NOT in this category (use Fantasy instead)
